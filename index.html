How similar teams
handled volume growth

Published experiences with workflow, automation and e-filing. These are customer-specific results, not Meridian targets or Review Agent benchmarks.

BHG FinancialLender · more than 170,000 customers77%less time per SAR+

SAR work went from 90–180 minutes to 20–30 minutes. Integrated data, narrative templates, filing controls and AI file summaries supported the operating model.

Higher SAR volume with no added headcount, and no late or rejected filings. The overall reduction is not an isolated AI-effect estimate.

Canadian institutionMore than $15B in assets under management1,378analyst hours saved+

The institution reported 1,378 analyst hours saved in its first seven months and cleared more than 1,000 backlogged cases in a few months.

Filing automation addressed a specific STR bottleneck. The lesson for Meridian is to identify where work stalls before choosing the intervention.

Read the published story ↗
Grasshopper BankDigital commercial bank4.5×approximate asset scale+

Assets grew from about $200M to nearly $900M between 2022 and 2024. Hummingbird supported compliance operations through custom workflows and grouping related alerts into comprehensive cases.

The bank reported that compliance staffing did not grow at the same pace and that it had no rejected reports in three years. Asset growth is context, not a productivity result caused by Hummingbird.

Security & privacy overview ↗
No training on customer data+

Hummingbird says customer data is not used to train or fine-tune models.

Zero model-provider retention+

Hummingbird describes an OpenAI enterprise zero-retention agreement. Hummingbird continues to retain case data in its own platform, with some features using temporary storage in its secure cloud.

Processing in the United States+

Hummingbird’s published overview says all AI processing occurs in the United States.

Platform permissions and controls+

AI operates under Hummingbird’s standard security program, including SOC 2 Type 2, with access controls and reviewable actions.

Optional, with admin controls+

Admins can request that AI features be disabled. Permissions control access to some capabilities.

Evaluation before release+

Hummingbird describes accuracy evaluations, monitoring, red teaming and prompt-injection defenses.
